Under R-10 you are heating the sky. Here is the blow plan that fixes it once.

Depth, not labels

Old batts run R-3.2 per inch, blown fiberglass R-2.5, cellulose R-3.5. What is up there now is measured with a ruler, not the year on the wrapper.

The bag chart is real

Coverage drops fast as R climbs: 40 sq ft per bag at R-13, 9 at R-60. The back of the bag is the honest table nobody reads.

Seal first, blow second

The gaps around lights and hatches leak more than missing inches - and you cannot seal under 11 new inches of cellulose. The blower is free at 20 bags.
