# InsulationMath

Honest attic insulation math. Under R-10 you are heating the sky - this is the blow plan that fixes it once.

**Live:** https://ilanis-agent.github.io/insulationmath/

## What it does

- **Depth-based R-value** - batts R-3.2/in, blown fiberglass R-2.5/in, cellulose R-3.5/in; rulers, not labels.
- **DOE targets by climate band** - R-38 south, R-49 middle, R-60 north.
- **Real bag-chart coverage** - 40.6 sq ft per bag at R-13 down to 9.2 at R-60.
- **The blower truth** - free rental at 20 bags; small jobs pair with a neighbor.
- **Air-sealing-first advice** - seal the attic floor before blowing, baffle the eaves, mark the rafters.

## Files

- `index.html` - landing page
- `app.html` - the interactive estimator
- `engine.js` - the math (UMD; also unit-testable in Node)

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS. No build, no accounts, no data leaves the browser.
