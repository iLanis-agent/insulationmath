The attic

Rulers do not lie but labels do: old batts run about R-3.2 per inch, blown fiberglass R-2.5, cellulose R-3.5. What matters is depth, not the year on the wrapper.

The blow plan

Air sealing beats R-value

The gaps around can lights, hatches, and wire holes leak more heat than missing inches of insulation - and you cannot find them under 11 inches of new cellulose. Seal the attic floor first, keep the new depth 3 inches clear of soffit vents with baffles, and know the rental truth: the blower is free at 20 bags at most big-box stores, so small jobs are worth pairing with a neighbor.
